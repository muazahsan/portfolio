About Me

Engineering robotics & motion systems from first principles.

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) graduate from B.S. Abdur Rahman Crescent Institute of Science & Technology, Chennai, specializing in Industrial Robotics, Motion Control Algorithms, and Embedded Firmware.

My hands-on focus centers on the physical hardware floor: taking mathematical kinematics solvers (forward/inverse kinematics, Jacobian velocity mappings, polar coordinate translations) and implementing them directly on real industrial machines. At Kelvin6k, I develop and deploy software stacks across multiple industrial robotic platforms—most notably the Atom V2 4-axis palletizer robot (tuned down to 0.48° precision), mobile coordinate cylindrical 3DCP printers (Atom V1), and high-reach SCARA systems (Aton).

Beyond industrial robotics, I design deterministic embedded systems (ESP32, STM32, Arduino) and bridge physical machines with responsive digital control applications—from standalone Python/Qt ERPs to edge-AI emergency navigation prototypes validated with the Kerala Police.
